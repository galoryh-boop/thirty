# Thirty — the book

**Three Simple Habits. Thirty Days. A Body That Works.**
by Galor Yahood

The method, the reasoning and the research behind the Thirty app: why three habits and not twelve, why a pass mark below perfect, what counts for sleep, movement and nutrition, the six movements and the rule that makes you stronger, a month followed day by day, and an honest chapter on what thirty days will and will not do — with every study cited and its limits stated.
