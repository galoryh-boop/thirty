# Thirty — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 September 2026_

Thirty is a personal habit tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

## What Thirty stores
Everything you enter — daily habits (sugar, water), sleep, workouts and sets, comments, and progress photos — is stored **only on your iPhone**, and mirrored to your Apple Watch when one is paired. Thirty has no user accounts, no server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Apple Health
With your permission, Thirty **reads** two things from Apple Health: sleep analysis (to fill in last night's hours) and the sleeping breathing-disturbances score. Thirty never writes to Health and never shares Health data with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps › Thirty. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold.

## Apple Intelligence
On phones that support it, short workout notes are generated by Apple's on-device model. This happens entirely on your phone; no data is sent to Apple or to us.

## Photos and camera
Progress photos are stored in the app's private folder on your phone, excluded from iCloud backup, and never appear on the widget or watch.

## Notifications
Reminders and rest-timer alerts are scheduled locally on your device.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Starting a new 30-day cycle does not delete anything.

## Children
Thirty is not directed at children under 13.
